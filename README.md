# Frosty Wheels – Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview
Frosty Wheels is a business intelligence project developed using Power BI to analyze business performance across sales, customers, products, inventory, deliveries, and payments.

The goal of this project is to transform business data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Performance Overview
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Product and Category Performance

### 2. Customer Analysis
- Total Customers
- Customer Revenue
- Average Spend per Customer
- Repeat Customer Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Customer Revenue by City

### 3. Product Analysis
- Total Products
- Best-Selling Product
- Total Quantity Sold
- Product Revenue
- Product-wise Sales Comparison

### 4. Inventory Analysis
- Total Products in Stock
- Total Stock Quantity
- Total Inventory Value
- Low Stock Products
- Warehouse-wise Inventory Distribution

### 5. Delivery & Payment Analysis
- Total Deliveries
- Delivered Orders
- Pending Deliveries
- Successful Payments
- Delivery Status Distribution
- Payment Status Analysis

## Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **DAX** – Measures and calculated columns
- **MySQL** – Data storage and data analysis

## Key Features
- Five interactive dashboard pages
- KPI cards for important business metrics
- Interactive slicers and filters
- Data-driven charts and visualizations
- Consistent dashboard design and navigation
